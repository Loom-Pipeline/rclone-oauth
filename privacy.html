Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

loom-backup is a personal backup tool used by one person, the owner of the Loom pipeline project, to copy their own files into their own Google Drive with rclone.

What it accesses

It asks only for the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can read and write only the files and folders it has created itself. It cannot see anything else in the Drive.

What it collects and shares

Nothing. It collects no personal data, has no analytics, and sends no data to anyone other than Google Drive itself. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own machine, in rclone's local configuration.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
